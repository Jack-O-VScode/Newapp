Questions

Before you order

How fast is delivery?

Steam codes are usually sent within a few hours of payment clearing, often much sooner. Setup sessions are booked for a time that works in your timezone — most people get a slot the same day or the next.

Do you really ship worldwide?

Nothing physically ships. Both products are delivered over chat or a remote session, so there's no postage, no customs and no waiting on a parcel. Where you live only matters for picking a time slot.

Why is my Steam code region-locked?

That's how Valve issues them — a wallet code only redeems on an account registered to the same region. It isn't a restriction I add. Tell me your account's region when you order and you'll get a code that works.

Is it safe to let you remote into my PC?

You stay at the keyboard the whole time and watch everything I do. Sessions run over AnyDesk or TeamViewer, you approve the connection, and you can end it in one click at any point. Close anything private before we start — I'd rather you were careful.

What if something goes wrong?

Message me and I'll sort it. If a code doesn't redeem, I'll replace it or refund it. If you paid by PayPal Goods & Services you also have their dispute process as a backstop, which is exactly why I suggest it for overseas buyers.

Can I still buy through Carousell?

Yes, and if you're in Singapore that's often easiest — Carousell's Buyer Protection covers the transaction. This site exists mainly so buyers outside Singapore have a way to reach me.
