Questions

Before you order

How fast is delivery?

Steam codes are usually sent within a few hours of payment clearing, often much sooner. Setup sessions are booked for a time that works in your timezone — most people get a slot the same day or the next.

Do you really ship worldwide?

Nothing physically ships. Both products are delivered over chat or a remote session, so there's no postage, no customs and no waiting on a parcel. Where you live only matters for picking a time slot.

How much do Steam codes cost?

Face value plus ten percent. Want S$10 of credit, you pay S$11 — no service fee, no markup that changes depending on how keen you seem. For codes in other regions I convert the cost first and apply the same ten percent, and I'll always confirm the exact total in chat before you pay anything.

Why is my Steam code region-locked?

That's how Valve issues them — a wallet code only redeems on an account registered to the same region. It isn't a restriction I add. Tell me your account's region when you order and you'll get a code that works.

What do I need for a WMMT6 setup?

Windows 10 or 11, Home or Pro — any version is fine. Add a controller or sim racing wheel if you want to use one, though a keyboard works too. Everything else is handled during the session.

How does booking a setup session work?

Pay, then pick a slot from my calendar — times are shown in your own timezone, so there's no maths to do (I'm in Singapore, UTC+8, if you're wondering). You'll get a confirmation with the connection details and I'll be there at the booked time. If nothing on the calendar suits you, message me and I'll open a slot.

What's your refund policy?

Setup sessions: full refund if the game turns out not to be compatible with your PC, and a full refund if I can't get it working because of an error on my end. Need to reschedule? Message me before your slot and I'll move it free of charge.
Steam codes: if a code doesn't redeem, I'll replace it or refund it.

Is it safe to let you remote into my PC?

Sessions run over Windows Quick Assist — Microsoft's own tool, already built into Windows 10 and 11. There's nothing to download and no third-party remote software to trust. You generate the code and give it to me, so the connection only happens because you started it. You stay at the keyboard watching everything I do and can end the session in one click at any point. Close anything private before we begin — I'd rather you were careful.

What if something goes wrong?

Message me and I'll sort it. If a code doesn't redeem, I'll replace it or refund it. If you paid by PayPal Goods & Services you also have their dispute process as a backstop, which is exactly why I suggest it for overseas buyers.

Can I still buy through Carousell?

Yes, and if you're in Singapore that's often easiest — Carousell's Buyer Protection covers the transaction. This site exists mainly so buyers outside Singapore have a way to reach me.
